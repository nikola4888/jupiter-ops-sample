Outside support sample: Jupiter

Based only on public materials, checked 9 October 2026. Not affiliated with Jupiter. Not a client engagement.

Written by Nikola

One help article, one troubleshooting tree, one escalation card. Every answer ends in a Jupiter page or a Jupiter door. Nothing here is advice.

1I staked JUP. Where are my rewards?

Jupiter pays Active Staking Rewards (ASR) once a quarter, in JUP, to people who staked during that quarter. Your share depends on how much you staked and for how long. Voting is not required.[1]

You have to claim it

ASR is not sent to you automatically. Each quarter has a claim window, and there is no fixed date for it: Jupiter announces it on its official channels when it opens. If the rewards page shows nothing, the window for that quarter has not started.[1] The docs name @jup_dao on X and the “Notify me” option on vote.jup.ag as the two ways to be told.[1]

You claim it somewhere other than where you staked

JUP is staked on vote.jup.ag. ASR is claimed at jup.ag/rewards, with the wallet you staked from.[2][1] You cannot claim while you are in the 7-day unstaking cooldown; finish it or cancel it first. A claim is an onchain transaction, so keep about 0.01 SOL in the wallet.[1]

Where the JUP lands

A claim made in the Rewards Hub is added to your staked balance, not your wallet balance. Check vote.jup.ag or jup.ag/portfolio. A claim handled by support through a ticket lands differently: at the main address of the wallet you staked with.[1]

The window closes

Claims stay open for a limited time. After the Rewards Hub closes, a support ticket is the last route until a final deadline. For the April to June 2026 round, claims opened on 8 July and the ticket route closed on 7 October 2026. Rewards left unclaimed after that are not distributed.[1][3]

The sentence support must not say

“The next ASR opens on [date].” The docs say there is no fixed date. A moderator who guesses a date has just written the opening line of every fake claim site that week.

2Troubleshooting tree

Five branches. Each one ends in a Jupiter link or a Jupiter door. Nothing on this page is a judgement call about someone's own transaction.

  1. “My swap keeps failing.”

    Link. The Support Hub answer on failed swaps: it should take no more than three tries; after that, open a ticket. The Spot FAQ covers “no routes found”.[4][5]

    Door. A ticket on support.jup.ag.[3]

    Not from support. Compensation. The Support Hub limits it to Ultra Mode users and specific cases, never for potential profit and loss, and that call belongs to the ticket.[4]

  2. “Is this the real token? It has a green check.”

    Link. For JUP itself: the JUP Token Overview, address copied from there, every character compared.[6] For anything else: the VRFD FAQ, which says verification confirms a token is the canonical version of its ticker, and is not an endorsement of the project, the team, or the token's quality or safety.[7]

    Not from support. “Verified, so it's safe.”

  3. “My Predict position is stuck after the event ended.”

    Link. The Predict FAQ, settlement section.[8]

    Door. The Predict FAQ is the one product page that sends tickets to Discord rather than support.jup.ag, and it lists what to include. I would follow it as written, and flag the difference upward.[8][3]

  4. “Can I scan to pay in my country?”

    Link. Spend Fees & Limits, which lists QR Pay countries and the cards it supports.[9] The on-chain QR payments @jup_mobile announced on 8 October are a different feature; when I checked, I found no docs page for them yet.[10][11]

    Not from support. A yes or no for a country that isn't on the page.

  5. “Someone DM'd me. They say I won, or they're support.”

    Link. For the Wallet birthday giveaway, the post itself: ten winners, picked at random, announced on 6 October.[12] For tokens or links that arrive unasked: the Mobile FAQ says not to interact with unknown tokens and to hide them.[13]

    Door. A ticket on support.jup.ag, or a report in the Discord listed on the Support Hub.[3][4]

    Not from support. A claim link, in any channel. I found no Jupiter page that states “support never DMs you first”. The sentence is mine, and getting it onto a Jupiter page would be the first docs fix I suggest.[11]

3Escalation card

Same rules as the main plan, short enough to pin.

  1. Answer from docs

    Anyone on the team, with a link every time

    • Where ASR is claimed, and where it lands
    • The 7-day unstake
    • Token addresses, copied from docs
    • What the verified badge means
    • Which countries QR Pay covers

  2. Raise it

    Through the ticket route the docs name for that product

    • A specific failed trade or compensation request
    • A Predict market stuck past resolution
    • Impersonators and fake giveaway winners
    • Docs that contradict each other

  3. Never promise

    Not by anyone, in any tone

    • Compensation
    • ASR dates or amounts
    • Yield
    • That a token is safe
