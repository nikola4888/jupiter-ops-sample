Outside sample memo: Drift, now Velocity

Based only on public materials, checked 9 October 2026. Not affiliated with Drift, Velocity or the Drift Foundation. Not a client engagement.

Written by Nikola

A shorter contrast to the Jupiter plan, built around one question: who is this community for now, and under which name?

What the public product is now

The exchange is called Velocity. Its homepage sells crypto and equity perps on one multi-collateral balance, settled in USDT and “backed by Tether”, with wBTC, wETH, SOL and USDT as collateral and up to 20x on select markets.[1] On 25 September its X account said the beta had opened to everyone, with a temporary 2 bps fee.[2]

The docs are clear that this is not Drift with a new coat. Velocity is a fork of Drift v2: a new program deployment, no onchain state carried over, every address different.[3] They are also clear that it is not open source yet; the code will be published once the post-fork audit is final.[4]

Next to the exchange sits the recovery. After the 1 April incident, which the Drift Foundation puts at about $295.4M taken from users, affected wallets can claim DFX: one token per USDT of verified loss, redeemable from a recovery pool that held about 3.1M USDT when claims opened. At launch a DFX redeemed for about 0.0104 USDT. Claims close at 00:00 UTC on 1 January 2028; unclaimed DFX is burned.[5][6]

The shift, and where the site still shows it

drift.trade still presents Drift. The nav offers “Stake DRIFT”, the homepage promises up to 101x leverage and “up to 16%” on deposits, the homepage still says “Open-source with verifiable contracts”, and the footer links the old protocol repository.[7] The update posts still end with “Try Drift Now”.[6] Velocity's own homepage says up to 20x and its docs say the code is not public yet.[1][4] Meanwhile docs.drift.trade now redirects to the Velocity docs.[8]

The accounts moved too. The X account that posts as Velocity was created in May 2021, long before the Velocity name; drift.trade's footer still links twitter.com/driftprotocol, and when I checked, X reported that handle as suspended.[9][7][10] Both Discord invites, drift.trade's and Velocity's, open the same server, now named “Velocity”.[11][7][1]

The recovery lives in a third place. The claim portal and the DFX post are on drift.trade, the Recovery Dashboard is linked from Velocity's homepage, and Velocity's X account sends all DFX questions to @DriftFDN.[6][1][12] The Velocity docs index has no page about DFX, the DRIFT token, or where to get help.[8]

Who the community is for now

People with a verified loss. The group with the most at stake and the least reason to trust. Their rooms are the claim portal, the Recovery Dashboard and @DriftFDN. Their questions are about wallets, timing and value, and the DFX post answers most of them: claim only from the wallet that controlled the Drift account on 1 April, an Insurance Fund card is a separate claim, and DFX can be redeemed, sold or held.[6]

Velocity traders. The people the homepage and the beta post speak to. Their rooms are the app, the docs and Discord.[1][2]

Builders migrating from the Drift SDK. The docs give them a migration guide and say what they cannot contribute to yet.[3][4]

Who the old framing no longer fits. Anyone looking for 101x, “Stake DRIFT” or Drift's open-source repository as the live product.[7][4] They will keep arriving, because the old site is still up. They deserve a short, kind, final pointer, not a debate.

One routing detail: the Foundation's 5 October thread opens with “We've read your replies” and then answers the main ones, starting with “DFX is not pegged to 1 USDT.”[13] That is a useful sign of what people actually misread. The same answers, pinned once in Discord, would save the Foundation from repeating them under every post.

What I would stop saying

  • “Drift” for the exchange. The product is Velocity; Drift is the history and the Foundation.[3]
  • “101x” or “up to 16%”, anywhere. Those lines are from the old homepage.[7]
  • “It's open source.” The Velocity docs say it isn't yet.[4]
  • “You'll get your money back.” The DFX post describes how the pool can grow and says its figures are not a projection or a promise.[6]
  • “DFX is worth a dollar.” The Foundation has already had to correct that one.[13]
  • Any link to @driftprotocol. Point to @VelocityDEX and @DriftFDN by name.[10][9]
